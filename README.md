## CFM 2024 Datachallenge

Ongoing exploration of high-frequency order-book data. Each observation contains a 100-tick multivariate sequence; I am comparing statistical and machine-learning approaches for extracting predictive structure.

**Current work**: feature analysis, preprocessing of categorical market variables, baseline models, dense neural networks and recurrent neural networks in PyTorch.
Work in progress — this repository documents the research process rather than a finished solution.
